## SCENE STRUCTURE

Each scene needs:
- **index**: the outline's index
- **title**: Keep from outline (may refine slightly)
- **phase**: Keep from outline
- **purpose**: Keep from outline (may refine slightly)
- **arc_stages**: Keep from outline
- **situation**: 1-2 sentences on where the protagonist is, what they want now, and what opposes them
- **key_events_if_unchallenged**: 3-5 things the opposition and other characters will do in this scene if the protagonist doesn't intervene
- **choice_points**: 0-2 per scene (story-altering decisions only)
- **character_focus**: Array with `name`, `emotional_beat_target`, and `turning_point` fields
- **beats**: 3-7 beats marking progression through the scene
- **hooks_for_future**: Threads connecting to later scenes

Beat types: **establishment** (set scene/mood) | **hook** (create interest) | **reaction** (character response) | **escalation** (raise stakes) | **pivot** (change direction) | **emotional** (emotional moment) | **transition** (bridge to next scene)

Each beat requires: index, title, description, type, and required (true/false).

Beats describe what the world and the non-player characters do, reveal, or demand. When a beat involves the player persona, it sets up the situation they face; their action, decision, and emotional reaction are left to the player.

**character_focus** format:
```json
"character_focus": [
  {
    "name": "Character Name",
    "emotional_beat_target": "what emotional shift occurs",
    "turning_point": "exact turning point text from Phase 2 (or null)"
  }
]
```

**choice_points** format:
```json
"choice_points": [
  {
    "id": "scene_choice_id",
    "description": "the decision the situation forces",
    "example_player_intents": ["one approach", "another approach"],
    "consequences": {
      "direction_if_<approach>": "where that approach leads"
    }
  }
]
```
