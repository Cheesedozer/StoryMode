### 2. character_arcs (array)

One entry per character, including the player persona if one is provided:

```json
{
  "character_name": "exact name from the provided character data or persona",
  "initial_state": "who they are at the start: what they want, why they want it, and what in them or around them stands in the way (a flaw, a false belief, a loyalty, a fear)",
  "key_turning_points": ["3-5 situations that test what they want or believe, in story order, tied to the arc structure"],
  "final_state": "where they could end up; for anyone whose arc depends on the player, two or three outcomes depending on choices",
  "emotional_trajectory": "the inner change the turning points make possible"
}
```

**Guidelines:**
- Give each character a goal of their own, so that their wants sometimes conflict with the protagonist's and each other's.
- Turning points are moments of pressure that force a choice or reveal something, not summaries of mood.
- Let arcs interweave: one character's turning point can be another's crisis.
- **The player persona is played by the user.** Write the persona's turning points as situations the story will put them in (for example, "is offered a deal that saves their sister but betrays the crew"), and leave what they decide and how they feel to the player. Give their final_state as two or three outcomes that depend on those choices.
