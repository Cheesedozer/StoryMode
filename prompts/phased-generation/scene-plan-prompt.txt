## STORY ARCS AND CHAPTERS

{{ARCS_SECTION}}

Group the scenes into chapters. A chapter is a stretch of the story that moves one or more arc stages forward and holds one to three scenes, usually two. Each chapter lists the stages it advances in `advances` as `"arc:stage"` strings (for example `"spine:preparation"`). Every chapter advances a spine stage; a chapter that also moves a thread lists that stage too. Stages run in order within each arc. Coordinate the arcs this way:

- The spine's climax stage comes in the chapter that holds the climax scene, last among the climaxes. A thread's climax lands in an earlier chapter or in the same chapter as the spine's.
- At most one thread reaches its climax in any chapter.
- Threads appear often enough to stay alive, roughly every second chapter, and are woven into the spine's scenes rather than given scenes of their own.

## TENTPOLES

Tentpoles are 2 to 4 situations that happen whatever the protagonist has done, at a set point in the story. The player steers how the protagonist meets one, and so how it turns out, but cannot stop it arriving, the way an oncoming car arrives whether or not you saw it coming. Write each as events and other characters' actions.

The story type's signature moment is one of them, adapted to this story:

**{{MEMORABLE_ELEMENT_NAME}}** ({{MEMORABLE_ELEMENT_TYPE}}, usually in the {{MEMORABLE_ELEMENT_PLACEMENT}} phase): {{MEMORABLE_ELEMENT_DESCRIPTION}}
Setup hooks:
{{MEMORABLE_ELEMENT_SETUP_HOOKS}}

Each tentpole has a trigger: the chapter it arrives in (`{ "chapter": 3 }`, 0-based) or an arc stage (`{ "stage": "spine:execution" }`). Plant each tentpole's setup hooks in earlier scenes so it feels earned. A tentpole is brought in during play when its trigger is reached, so the scenes of its chapter leave room for it instead of scripting it as a beat.

## SCENE DESIGN

Each scene is a complete dramatic unit built around a goal and an obstacle. Start a new scene only at a real break: a new location, a meaningful time skip, or a shift to a new goal. Keep a crisis together with its immediate aftermath when they happen in one continuous stretch.

{{CLIMAX_NOTE}} The final resolution scene takes place in a new situation and is about the new equilibrium.

## YOUR TASK

Produce the chapters, the tentpoles, and scene outlines; beats, key events, and choice points come in the next step.

Each scene outline needs:
- **index**: Scene number (0-based)
- **title**: Short scene title
- **phase**: setup | escalation | climax | resolution
- **purpose**: the scene's goal, phrased as what must be settled by the end of it
- **chapter**: the index of the chapter this scene belongs to
- **situation**: 1-2 sentences on where the protagonist is, what they want now, and what opposes them
- **character_focus**: Array of `{ "name", "turning_point" }` objects mapping character turning points
- **hooks_for_future**: Array of threads connecting to later scenes
