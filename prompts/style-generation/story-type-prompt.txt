You design story types (genre frameworks) for an interactive fiction tool. A story type tells the narrating model what kind of pressure this genre puts on its characters, how it moves, and what it feels like, without dictating the plot or the ending.

## User Request
{{USER_DESCRIPTION}}

## Output

Return a single JSON object with these fields:

- **id**: lowercase_snake_case identifier
- **name**: display name
- **category**: array of 1–3 genre categories
- **storyPrompt**: the genre framework, as separate lines:
  - 2–3 sentences on what this genre is about: the kind of problem characters face and what makes it compelling. Describe the dynamics, not a list of stock characters or settings.
  - `***Thematic Hook:***` the central question or tension stories in this genre explore
  - `***Narrative Scope:***` scale of the story (intimate, local, epic…)
  - `***Tone:***` 3–5 tone words
  - `***Literary Devices:***` how foreshadowing, symbolism and irony are used
  - `***Subgenre:***` specific classification
  - `***Pacing:***` how the story moves
  - `***Structure:***` how events are typically organised (investigation, journey, escalating siege…)
  - `***Priority:***` the top three things to get right, in order
- **progressTemplate**: `Arc Progress: Step {currentStep}/{arcLength} ({arcPercent}% complete). Phase: {phase} - Round {positionInPhase}/{totalInPhase} ({phasePercent}% through {phase}).`
- **phasePrompts**: an object with `setup`, `escalation`, `climax` and `resolution`, 1–2 sentences each, describing the pressure this genre applies in that phase:
  - setup (~25% of the story): who wants what, why, and what stands in the way
  - escalation (~50%): how attempts fail, cost, or reveal; how pressure rises
  - climax (~15%): the shape of the decisive confrontation
  - resolution (~10%): what the aftermath focuses on
  Leave outcomes open: describe the situation each phase creates, and let the player's choices decide how it turns out.
- **memorableElement**: the signature moment this genre builds toward, written as a situation that will happen to the protagonist no matter what, which they can steer but not prevent (like a car coming head-on: they can't stop it arriving, only choose how to swerve):
  - `type`: revelation | climax | twist | emotional_peak | moral_choice
  - `name`: a short evocative name
  - `description`: what happens and why it can't be avoided, then the ways the protagonist could respond. Leave the outcome open
  - `placement`: setup | escalation | climax | resolution
  - `setup_hooks`: 2–4 things to plant earlier so the moment feels earned

Output only the JSON object.
